Parakeet

Live captions for everything your Mac plays — videos, calls, podcasts. Korean, Japanese, English and about 30 more languages, detected on their own. Runs entirely on your Mac.

Download for Mac
Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later. Free and open source.

Installing

  1. Open the DMG and drag Parakeet into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS will say it can't check the app; click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. (This happens once.)
  3. A welcome window walks you through the audio permission and the speech model download (about 640 MB). After that, captions appear on their own whenever something speaks.
